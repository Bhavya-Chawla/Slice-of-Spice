# Slice-of-Spice
. Restaurant management system increases operational efficiency through direct interaction with the customer. The aim of the project is to develop a management system which is easily accessible to the customers.
The user can access the management system by entering his details such as his name and contact number. The menu is then generated to the user which includes a variety of options including starters, beverages, main course and desserts. Each menu item has a name along with its price. The user can then select the item of his/her choice from a vast range of options along with its quantity. As soon as item is selected it is added to the order. The customer can view his order at any point of time which shows the list of choices selected along with its quantity and price. An item can be added, deleted or have its quantity changed by the customer after reviewing the order. The user is then shown the total price of his order. After the user has placed his order, he is then presented with the special offers or discounts offered depending upon his choice of payment method. After selecting the preferred payment method, the bill is presented which includes the necessary details such as customer name, name of the restaurant, data and time of the bill being printed, order and the total cost.
